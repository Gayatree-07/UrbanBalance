How to read these results
UrbanBalance uses a simplified, transparent scenario model to compare relative outcomes. Its values are based on stated project assumptions; they are not measured forecasts or engineering guarantees.

MODEL TRANSPARENCY

What drives the simulation?

Heat exposure
Starts from a base heat index and adjusts for impervious area, preserved-tree benefits, and selected interventions.

Runoff pressure
Shows remaining runoff as a percentage of the unmitigated runoff for the selected development strategy. Lower is better.

Shade coverage
Estimates effective tree shade plus shade infrastructure, relative to the modeled pedestrian area.

Resilience index
Combines heat resilience (40%), water resilience (40%), and shade coverage (20%). Development capacity is shown separately.

All areas, costs, rainfall, benefits, and weighting values are scenario assumptions for exploration—not universal standards or real-world predictions.
